The single line of text a smart fridge sends its owner at 3am — 140 characters or fewer.

Running `main.ts` writes the line to `output/entry.txt`:

> 3am again. The light's on, the milk's gone off, and the leftovers have unionized. Go back to bed. I'll guard the cheese.
